v1.4.0
- Defending lords now fortify against you. Attack a lord holding ground in the field and he digs in: his Engineering decides what his men can build, his purse how much of it, obstacles first. His barricades stop your men and impale your horses, and his archers crew his engines.
- Your scouts report what he has built before you commit, in the fortify menu and its tooltip.
- Lords never build the archer platform, and parties without a proper commander never fortify. Turn it off with ai_fortifications=0 or scale it with ai_scale.
- Engines now keep asking their owner for a crew until one arrives, instead of asking once. An army that has not formed up yet, which is the usual case for the enemy, no longer leaves its engines standing idle, and your own engines recover when their crew is killed.
- Works are only placed on ground that is flat enough, on the navigation mesh, and connected by a walkable path to the army that owns them, so nothing is built on a cliff or across a ravine where nobody can reach it.

v1.3.0
- Engineering requirements: each work needs an engineer of a certain level, and every further copy needs more (eng_* and eng_step in settings.txt). The menu shows the level next to the price and says who your engineer is.
- Companion engineers: the highest Engineering in your party counts, so a companion can do the engineering for you.
- Your engineer gains Engineering experience for every work bought (eng_xp_per_1000 in settings.txt).
- Placement cards now wrap into rows of eight instead of running off the screen.

v1.2.1
- Fixed a crash (Collection was modified) when a horse died on the barricade spikes: the spike check now applies its hits after walking the agent list.
- Any fault in the mod's per-tick checks during a battle is now logged to errors.txt instead of ending the game.

v1.2.0
- New "Fortify your position" menu in the encounter screen: one line per work with the count you hold, the cap and the next price, a running total of what you paid and what you have left, and a "Take the works down" option that refunds everything.
- Works can now be bought several times per battle: up to 3 barricade lines, 2 ballistas, 2 catapults, 4 arrow stockpiles and 1 platform (max_* in settings.txt).
- Each further copy of a work costs more: 50% of the base price on top of the previous one by default (price_step in settings.txt).
- Every copy gets its own placement card and ghost, numbered when there is more than one, with sensible default spots spread along the front and flanks.

v1.1.0
- New "Stock arrows" option: two arrow barrels. Archers whose quivers drop below a threshold (refill_below in settings.txt) walk over and refill.
- New "Build an archer platform" option: a raised 12 x 8 m timber deck on posts with a full-width ramp, with its own walkable navmesh so formations can be ordered onto it.
- The platform navmesh piece is copied into Native/NavMeshPrefabs the first time a platform is built; the engine only reads that folder.
- Safety net around crew assignment so a machine without an AI object cannot end the battle.
- Optional debug=1 setting records game error stacks during a battle to debug.txt.

v1.0.0
- Initial release.
- Encounter menu options to buy barricades, a ballista and a catapult before a field battle (open-terrain battles only).
- Ghost previews and cards during deployment: click a card or press P, move with the mouse, rotate with the arrow keys, Enter to place.
- Barricades carry a blocker navmesh so the AI paths around them; horses that charge them are impaled.
- Ballista and catapult are crewed by your archers and fire on their own; a catapult left without a crew reloads itself.
- Prices, crew, ammo and keys are set in settings.txt.
