Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.3
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8. Works with or without War Sails.


Fixes
-----
- War Sails: naval patrols, fleets and naval armies work again. The mod's
  four campaign AI models replaced the DLC's own naval versions outright, so
  ships lost their naval logic; coastal patrols, for example, sat frozen at
  the shore. The models now wrap whatever model was registered before them
  (the DLC's, vanilla's or another mod's) and only adjust its answers, so
  naval behaviour and other mods' AI changes are kept.
- The mod now loads after War Sails when the DLC is installed.
- The log now names the models the mod is wrapping at startup, so a log
  shows at a glance whether the DLC's AI is underneath.


Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.2
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Fixes
-----
- Kingdoms no longer all go on the defensive at once. War exhaustion counted
  every enemy's full strength, so both sides of a war could count as
  outmatched whenever either was also fighting elsewhere; on maps with many
  kingdoms nearly every realm sat on ForcePeace and wars stalled. An enemy
  fighting several wars now counts with only its share of its strength.
- A large realm is no longer worn out by the same three raided villages as a
  small one: the raid threshold now also scales with the number of villages.
- A ruler granting a newly taken fief to one of his own clans is no longer
  logged as a capture or remembered as a lost fief, so a realm can no longer
  set out to reclaim a castle it already holds. Saves carrying such a claim
  drop it automatically.

Added setting: ExhaustionRaidedFraction.


Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.1
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Changes
-------
- Armies now commit to a siege rather than switching between nearby targets.
- Kingdoms hold a defensive footing steadily instead of alternating between
  defending and attacking.
- Army roles are held for a minimum period, so armies stop changing course.
- Siege judgement counts only a rival kingdom's relieving force, not troops
  already inside the walls.
- A failed siege is remembered per kingdom, so an army does not turn straight
  back to the same target.
- Threat assessment is now consistent from one check to the next.
- Strategic memory is tidied for armies that no longer exist.

Added settings: SiegeAbandonConfirmationHours, RoleMinimumHoldHours,
UrgentDefenseLatchHours.


Strategic Campaign AI - v0.3.0
==============================

Requires Mount & Blade II: Bannerlord v1.4.8.


Changes
-------
- Improved kingdom target selection so armies commit to reachable frontline
  objectives instead of drifting between them.
- Rebalanced war goals, war exhaustion and peace pressure, so kingdoms take up
  distinct postures and contest border fiefs properly.
- Armies disengage from sieges they cannot win.
- Better garrison support for fiefs that are genuinely under threat.
- Improved raid response and defence of newly captured fiefs.
- Interception and avoidance behaviour reworked.
- Significant performance improvements to campaign AI scoring.
- Stability fixes.
- Strategic memory now persists across save and load.


Added
-----
- ModuleData/settings.txt - documented settings for tuning the AI, applied on
  restart. No rebuild needed.
- Optional diagnostic log for troubleshooting. Set VerboseLogging = true in
  settings.txt to write to Documents\Mount and Blade II Bannerlord\StrategicCampaignAI.log.


Notes
-----
Safe to add or remove mid-save.
Delete any previous version of the module folder before installing.
